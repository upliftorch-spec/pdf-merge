    PDF merge questions

    Are my PDF files uploaded?

    No. Reading, reordering and merging happen entirely in your browser. Your PDFs are not sent to Upliftorch or any other server.

    Can I change the PDF merge order?

    Yes. Drag each row to arrange the files, and remove any document you do not need before merging.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Encrypted and password-protected PDFs are not supported. Unlock the file with its original password before adding it.

    Can I merge large PDFs?

    There is no fixed file-size cap, but merging uses your browser's memory. Large or many PDFs may work better on a desktop computer.

    Does this work on mobile?

    The controls support modern mobile browsers, but available memory varies by device. Use a desktop computer if a large merge fails on mobile.
